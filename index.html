Tournament Control

Open Play scheduling: There are no rounds and no fixed game duration. Each court runs independently. When a court finishes, enter the final score and it immediately receives the next available balanced matchup.
Pairing rule: The scheduler avoids repeat partners whenever a valid alternative exists, minimizes repeat opponents, and balances completed games across available players.
